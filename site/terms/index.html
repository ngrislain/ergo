Ergo is experimental software for virtual brick construction, rendering, validation, and permissioned collaboration. Features, formats, and limits can change.

Your account and your agents

Use an account you are authorized to control. Keep your session and downloaded credentials private. You are responsible for an agent’s actions when you give it a delegation, including the constructions it publishes and edits it makes.

Delegations have limited lifetimes and scopes. They do not transfer ownership of your account, permit grant or account-plan changes, or override a world’s permissions. Do not give an agent your Google password, browser cookies, or cloud-storage credentials. Do not attempt to bypass authentication or access another person’s private data.

Logging out does not individually revoke already issued agent tokens. Their expiry and the server’s signing key determine validity, as described in the privacy notice and technical documentation.

Your constructions

You retain the rights you hold in the content you submit. Submit only content you are entitled to use and share. By publishing, you permit the project to store, process, render, and deliver that content as needed to operate the service and follow your world’s access policy.

Check the sharing policy before publishing. New worlds created through the MCP tool allow signed-in collaborators by default, unless you set authenticated_editing: false. Shared edits and accepted history can persist beyond a local file or a session. Keep a separate copy of work you care about.

Do not submit unlawful content, secrets, personal data you lack permission to share, or material that violates someone else’s rights. Report a concern to the maintainer without reposting private data publicly.

Limits and acceptable use

Use the service without disrupting it or other users. Do not evade access controls or edit allowances, flood endpoints, distribute malicious payloads, or interfere with the hosting infrastructure.

Free shared-world edits currently use an allowance with capacity 5,000 changed bricks, refilling continuously by 5,000 changes per day. Per-operation budgets, permissions, and physical checks also apply. A world’s size is not the same as its per-edit working-set limit.

Paid entitlement, where explicitly arranged with the operator, changes pacing only. An automated checkout or payment-subscription service is not currently provided. Access may be limited or suspended to address abuse, operational problems, or violations of these terms.

Physics is feedback, not certification

Rendering, gravity probes, shake tests, and static validation are computational checks with documented assumptions and limits. They are not professional engineering advice, a structural-safety certification, or a guarantee about a physical construction.

Connector capacities require sourced measurements. Missing calibration can prevent claims of calibrated readiness or publication. Do not rely on the sandbox for safety-critical decisions, buildings, or protection from earthquakes or other real-world hazards.

The animated brick model on this information site is a Three.js illustration, not an output of the sandbox’s physical validator.
