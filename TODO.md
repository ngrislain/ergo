# Implementation backlog

`docs/` describes implemented, supported behavior. Unimplemented designs and
work items live in `todo/`; completing an item includes code, verification and
updating the relevant current documentation.

- [Rendering and memory](todo/rendering.md): streamed pages, workers, occlusion,
  simplification, cache policies and performance studies.
- [Physical fidelity and solvers](todo/physics.md): measured capacities,
  directional envelopes, general equilibrium, larger islands and destruction.
- [Editing and navigation](todo/editing.md): gizmos, precision positioning,
  complete contextual CAD editing, history and gameplay triggers.
- [Assets and imports](todo/assets.md): bulk conversion, catalogue discovery,
  specialty parts/materials and explicit import positioning.
- [Collaboration and scale-out](todo/collaboration.md): distributed publication,
  mechanical boundaries, recovery and workload qualification.
- [Operations and product](todo/operations.md): billing, revocation, resource
  controls, integration coverage and optional registry publication.
- [Research for those plans](todo/research.md): references that are not implemented
  algorithms or product guarantees.

For current architecture, formats, tools and operations, start with
[the technical documentation](docs/README.md).
